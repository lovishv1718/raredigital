COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about our services, setup timelines, and performance guarantees.

Which is better for my business: Meta Ads or Google Ads?

It depends on what you sell. Google Ads target "active intent" (people searching for a specific product or service). Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram) target "passive interest" (demographics and behaviors). We typically recommend a combination of both for maximum growth.

How much should my daily advertising budget be?

We recommend starting with a minimum ad spend of ₹500 to ₹1,000 per day per platform to gather enough data. As we find profitable campaigns, we scale the budget accordingly to maximize your ROI.

Are the ad creative costs included in your pricing?

Yes, in our Growth and Pro plans, we design all ad copy, image graphics, and even edit short video ads. In the Starter plan, we focus on ad copy and strategy using assets you provide.

How soon will we get leads once the ads go live?

Most clients see their first leads within 48 hours of campaigns going active. We build custom landing pages optimized for conversions, ensuring high-quality leads from day one.

How do you prevent wasting budget on fake clicks or spam leads?

We set up strict targeting filters, negative keyword lists, and custom audience exclusions. We also integrate instant WhatsApp lead qualification so you only spend time on real buyers.

Do you offer paid advertising services in Ludhiana?

Yes, we manage local PPC campaigns for clients across Punjab. You can read details about our localized campaigns on our Ludhiana digital marketing portal.
