STRATEGY COMPARISON

Why Invest in Entity-First SEO?

Understand how our SEO approach compares to other channels and standard agency models.

SEO vs. Google Ads

Google Ads (PPC) deliver immediate visibility and fast lead generation, but traffic stops the moment your budget runs out. SEO is a long-term asset; it requires upfront time investment (typically 3-6 months) but significantly lowers your Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) over time by capturing free, high-intent organic clicks.

SEO vs. Social Media

Social media is powerful for brand awareness and engagement, relying on interruption-based marketing. SEO targets users who are actively searching for a solution to their immediate problem. This fundamental difference in user intent is why local SEO historically drives significantly higher conversion rates for service-based businesses.

In-House vs. Agency

Hiring a full-time, in-house SEO specialist typically requires a substantial salary, plus additional budget for premium tools (Ahrefs, Semrush) and content writers. Partnering with a specialized agency provides you with an entire fractional team of technical experts, copywriters, and developers for a fraction of the cost, eliminating training time and software overhead.
