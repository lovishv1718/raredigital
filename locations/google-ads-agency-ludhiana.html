STRATEGY COMPARISON

Why Invest in Managed PPC?

Understand how Google Ads management compares to other channels and models.

Google Ads vs. SEO

Google Ads (PPC) delivers immediate search visibility and lead generation, placing your brand at the top of results instantly. Traffic begins as soon as the campaign is active. SEO, while highly sustainable and lowering cost-per-lead over time, is a long-term asset that takes 3 to 6 months to mature. A hybrid setup provides both instant leads and long-term organic authority.

Google Ads vs. Social Ads

Social media ads (Meta, Instagram) rely on interruption marketing, capturing users who are browsing social feeds. Google Ads targets search intent, capturing users who are actively searching for a solution, service, or product. This high search intent historically translates to significantly higher conversion rates and ready-to-buy customer generation.

Dedicated Landing Page vs. Website

Sending paid traffic to your standard website homepage is a common cause of wasted budget. Main websites feature multiple links, menus, and distractions that lower conversion rates. We design dedicated, fast, single-focus landing pages built strictly to capture the user's inquiry, doubling lead volume from the same ad budget.
