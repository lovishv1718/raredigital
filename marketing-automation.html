What is marketing automation?

Marketing automation uses software to automatically send messages, emails and WhatsApp replies to your leads based on their actions — without you lifting a finger.

Do I need to have a CRM already?

No. If you don't have one, we recommend and set up a simple CRM as part of the onboarding process.

Will the messages sound robotic?

No. We write all automation copy in your brand's voice so every message sounds personal and human.

How quickly can automation be set up?

A basic WhatsApp automation can be live within 3-5 days. Full sequences with CRM integration typically take 1-2 weeks.

Can I edit the messages myself later?

Yes. We hand over full access and provide a walkthrough so you can update messages whenever needed.
